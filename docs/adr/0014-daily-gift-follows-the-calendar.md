# Ежедневный подарок follows the calendar and a miss does not move it

Дом offers Подарок once on each real local day. The gift is the next step on a fixed coin track, and the track repeats after the last step. Skipping a day does not restart the track and does not jump ahead: the next day the app is opened still pays the next unclaimed step. This is not an Игровой день (ADR-0007) and not Пособие. It amends ADR-0010: these coins are an extra source, beside a finished Задание and вклад interest.

A missed day could have reset the track, or advanced one step per missed day. A reset takes away a place the child had reached. Skipping ahead pays for days they were not here. The place moves only when a gift is taken.

After the gift is taken, Подарок stays off Дом until the next local date. Демо-режим uses the same calendar. Удалить профиль and a demo reset clear the place with the profile.
