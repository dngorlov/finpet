# Unconfirmed plan drops mood

An open План that is never confirmed drops Счастье by 10 when the Игровой день ends, and Итоги дня says why. A closed План still lets the day end with no promise: the drop starts only after «Планирование бюджета», or from the first day in Демо-режим, and not on the day that lesson itself ends the day. A draft that was never confirmed counts as no plan. Spending past Желаемые stays a separate drop and only applies once a plan exists.
