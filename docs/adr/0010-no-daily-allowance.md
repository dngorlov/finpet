# No daily Пособие

New coins come from a completed Задание, or from the interest when a Вклад matures. The Стартовый бюджет stays a one-time grant at profile creation. A pinned Урок's max reward is 30 or 35, so finishing the lesson that ends the day covers that day's Счета the way the old +20 stipend did. Mini-games stay at 10 or 15.
