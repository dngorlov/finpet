# Daily meter drop, cancelled by a shop purchase

Superseded by [ADR-0013](./0013-daily-drop-always-lands.md).

Every Игровой день takes 15 Сытость and 15 Счастье. A Магазин purchase paid from Баланс cancels the drop for each meter that purchase feeds. The per-item chip «не купишь: −15» treated the drop as the cost of skipping that row, while two Счастье chips still meant one drop, and Итоги дня mentioned the drop only after it had landed. The drop belongs to the day. Дом states it in the «День N» tip and Магазин states it under the tabs. План does not repeat it. Итоги дня and Итоги say whether a purchase cancelled it. A Цель bought from Копилка does not cancel it. Spending past the Желаемые promise stays a separate Счастье drop.
