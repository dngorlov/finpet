# Daily meter drop always lands; a purchase only offsets it

Every Игровой день takes 15 Сытость and 15 Счастье whether or not the child bought anything. A purchase adds its own meter gain when it is bought, and that gain is the offset — it does not cancel the day's drop. Итоги дня always shows the drop. Дом and Магазин say a shop purchase compensates for it. Spending past the Желаемые promise and an unconfirmed План stay separate Счастье drops on top.

Supersedes ADR-0009.
